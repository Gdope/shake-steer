The drinks

Ten signatures,
seventy ways to say yes

These ten are the house creations we are known for. Behind them sits a full book of classics, spritzes, tiki serves and alcohol-free drinks — all of it in the PDF at the bottom of this page.

Signature creations

The house list

Rewritten twice a year around what is in season. Every one of them can be batched for large events without losing a thing.

Whisky

Velvet Sovereign

Aged whisky softened with fig and vanilla, finished with a whisper of cherrywood smoke released at the table.

Gin

Gilded Plum

Gin stirred down with plum liqueur and golden chamomile, bright as a late-afternoon sun and twice as easy to finish.

Mezcal

Rosé Noir

Smoked mezcal, rose and pink grapefruit over a long block of clear ice — floral at the front, quietly smoky at the end.

Gin

Adriatic Mist

Coastal gin, sea-salt tonic and a cordial of green olive and rosemary. Clean, saline and impossible to drink slowly in August.

Champagne

Bordeaux Ember

Blackcurrant and burnt orange under a slow pour of champagne, served in a chilled flute with a single gold leaf.

Vodka

The Violet Hour

Vodka washed with violet and blackberry, lengthened with sparkling wine and served the colour of the sky ten minutes after sunset.

Alcohol-free

Garden Room

Cucumber, green apple and lovage with a verjus cordial and soda. Every bit as considered as the drinks with spirits in them.

Rum

Amber & Anise

Dark rum, roasted pineapple and star anise, poured tall over crushed ice with a cinnamon-sugar rim.

Tequila

Midnight Harbour

Blanco tequila with blue butterfly pea, lime and a salted honey cordial that shifts colour as it dilutes.

Champagne

Fête Royale

Elderflower, white peach and champagne with a sugar cube soaked in bitters — our welcome drink of choice for weddings.
