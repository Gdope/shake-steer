Who we are

A bar that arrives, and an evening that stays with people

Shake&Steer builds a complete cocktail bar inside your venue — the counter, the glassware, the ice, the garnishes and the people behind it. Everything is prepared in our own workshop and assembled on site hours before your first guest arrives.

We work with a short, seasonal list of house creations and a long book of classics, so every menu we write feels like it belongs to that one evening and to nobody else.

What we do

Occasions we pour for

Five kinds of evening, one standard. Every format below can be scaled from twenty guests in a living room to five hundred under a marquee.

Weddings

A signature couple's cocktail, a welcome pour for the aperitif hour and a bar that keeps the floor busy until the last song.

Private parties

Rooftops, villas, gardens and apartments. We bring the bar, the ice and the music-friendly volume of service a good party needs.

Corporate events

Launches, conferences and client evenings — branded serves, fast queues and an invoice your finance team will accept without a sigh.

Birthdays

From a milestone dinner for twelve to a warehouse party for two hundred, built around the drinks the guest of honour actually likes.

Celebrations

Christenings, anniversaries, engagements and homecomings — including gentle, alcohol-free menus for family gatherings.

Something else in mind?

Product launches, film wraps, gallery openings, private tastings. If there are people and a reason, we can build a bar for it.

From the list

Four of our signatures

Ten house creations sit alongside roughly seventy classics. Here are four that tend to disappear first.

Whisky

Velvet Sovereign

Aged whisky softened with fig and vanilla, finished with a whisper of cherrywood smoke released at the table.

Gin

Gilded Plum

Gin stirred down with plum liqueur and golden chamomile, bright as a late-afternoon sun and twice as easy to finish.

Mezcal

Rosé Noir

Smoked mezcal, rose and pink grapefruit over a long block of clear ice — floral at the front, quietly smoky at the end.

Champagne

Bordeaux Ember

Blackcurrant and burnt orange under a slow pour of champagne, served in a chilled flute with a single gold leaf.

How it works

Four steps from first message to last pour

Talk it through

Tell us the date, the place and roughly how many people. We reply with availability and a few honest questions.

Design the menu

We propose a menu built around your taste and your venue, including alcohol-free serves that nobody feels short-changed by.

We set up

The bar, ice, glassware, garnishes and team arrive early, install quietly and leave the space exactly as we found it.

You celebrate

You spend the evening with your guests. We take care of everything on the other side of the counter.

Tell us about your evening

Dates go quickly in high season. Send us the details and we will come back with a proposal and a quote — usually within two working days.
