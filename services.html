Services & packages

A bar built around
the evening you are planning

Five kinds of event, three packages and a quote written for your date, your venue and your guest list. No fixed price list, because no two evenings are the same.

What we cater

Five kinds of evening

Each one has its own rhythm. The list under every card is what a typical booking of that type includes.

Weddings

From the welcome pour as guests arrive to the last round after the band packs up. We coordinate directly with your planner, caterer and venue so the bar never becomes one more thing for you to manage.

  • A signature cocktail created for the couple
  • Welcome drinks and champagne service for toasts
  • Printed menus matched to your stationery
  • Site visit and timeline planning with your venue

Private parties

House parties, rooftops, villas and garden evenings. A compact bar that fits through a normal doorway, a menu of four or five drinks, and a team that knows when to pour quickly and when to slow down.

  • Compact modular bar for tight spaces
  • Menu tailored to the host's taste
  • Glassware, ice and garnishes included
  • Full clean-down before we leave

Corporate events

Launches, conferences, staff parties and client evenings. We plan for peak demand — the fifteen minutes after a keynote ends — so nobody spends the networking hour in a queue.

  • Branded serves, garnishes and menu cards
  • Throughput planning for large arrivals
  • Alcohol-free menu of equal standard
  • Company invoice, insurance and licences in order

Birthdays

Milestone dinners, surprise parties and long nights in rented spaces. We build the list around what the guest of honour actually drinks, and keep one surprise back for the moment the cake arrives.

  • Menu built around the guest of honour
  • A birthday serve presented at the table
  • Flexible hours if the night runs long
  • Soft drinks and mocktails for every age

Celebrations

Christenings, anniversaries, engagements, graduations and homecomings — daytime events with mixed ages, where the alcohol-free half of the menu matters just as much as the rest.

  • Full alcohol-free menu, not an afterthought
  • Daytime service and family-friendly pacing
  • Quiet set-up around a ceremony or service
  • Smaller bar footprint for family homes

Something else

Gallery openings, film wraps, product launches, private tastings, cocktail masterclasses for a team. If there are people and a reason to raise a glass, we can build a bar for it.

  • Guided tastings and masterclasses
  • Bar takeovers and pop-ups
  • Bottled cocktails for gifting
  • Multi-day festivals and residencies

Good to know

Questions we are asked most

How far in advance should we book?

For weddings in high season, six to twelve months is comfortable. Smaller parties are often possible with a few weeks' notice — always ask, because cancellations happen.

Do you supply the alcohol?

Usually yes, and it is included in the quote. If your venue requires you to supply the drink yourself, we are happy to work as a dry-hire bar instead.

What do you need from the venue?

A level space of roughly three by two metres, one standard power socket and access for loading. No running water needed — we bring our own.

How many guests can you serve?

We plan one bartender for every fifty guests. Above three hundred we normally suggest a second bar rather than a longer queue.

Do you travel?

Yes. Travel within our standard service area is included; anything further is quoted openly, including accommodation where an early set-up requires it.

Tell us about your evening

Dates go quickly in high season. Send us the details and we will come back with a proposal and a quote — usually within two working days.
