Before you write

A first message with these five things lets us send back a real proposal instead of a list of questions.

  • The date, or the two or three dates you are considering
  • The venue, or at least the city and the kind of space
  • Roughly how many guests you expect
  • What kind of event it is, and how long the bar should run
  • Anything that matters to you: a favourite drink, a theme, a budget range
